The approach

Observation first.
Precise guidance.

Private teaching gives us time to understand your starting point. We look at your movement, breathing and how you organise effort. The practice develops through observation, conversation and consistent refinement.

Traditional yoga and modern movement inform the work. Depending on your needs, sessions can bring together asana, mobility, strength, pranayama, meditation or Yoga Nidra. The intention is to help you understand your practice and carry that understanding into your own time.

Ways we can work

Individual attention.
A clear direction.

Develop your practice

Establish foundations, refine a posture or sequence, or work through a period where your practice feels stuck.

Explore movement

Look more closely at mobility, strength and movement limitations, with adaptations that respond to your present capacity.

Work with breath & rest

Receive individual guidance in pranayama, meditation or Yoga Nidra, with time for questions and reflection.

If pain, injury or recovery is part of your enquiry, discuss it directly with Ankur before arranging sessions. The scope of yoga practice is agreed individually and does not replace medical care.

Do I need yoga experience?

No. Private sessions can establish a foundation or refine an existing practice. Your experience and goals guide the starting point.

Can we work online?

Yes. Enquire about private teaching in Mysore or live online. We will discuss the practical setup and which format suits the work.

How are sessions and fees arranged?

Share what you would like to work on, your preferred format and availability. Ankur will reply with the proposed approach, session arrangements and fees before you commit.
