01 / The breath

Pranayama classes in Mysore.
Build a steady practice.

Prana Urja is a four-week pranayama course rooted in the Hatha Yoga tradition. Each week builds gently on the last, with clear instruction in technique, posture and breathing rhythm, followed by time to notice the effects.

Yoga philosophy is explored through the practice: attention, habitual responses and the koshas, the traditional layers of human experience. The emphasis is on connecting these ideas with what you actually feel and observe.

Beginners receive a clear foundation, while practitioners and teachers can refine their understanding and approach. You learn how to adapt the practice and develop the confidence to continue with clarity and consistency.

  • 01Method: learn the structure and purpose of each technique.
  • 02Experience: notice the physical and mental response.
  • 03Independence: build a practice you can continue with clarity.

Can I learn pranayama as a beginner?

Yes. Prana Urja introduces techniques progressively, with clear explanations and time to observe your response. You do not need previous pranayama experience.

Can I attend the pranayama course online?

Yes. The one-month course is offered in Mysore and live online, Monday to Friday, 9:00–10:00 AM IST. Confirm the next start date, availability and fees with Ankur before enrolling.

What is different about this approach?

The emphasis is on understanding each technique through practice, with calm guidance and personal attention. Each week builds gently on the last so you can develop a steady practice to continue independently.

How is pranayama different from Yoga Nidra?

Pranayama works with breathing technique, rhythm and attention. Yoga Nidra is a guided rest and awareness practice, usually experienced lying down.
