01 / The method

How Mysore-style teaching works.

Ashtanga Vinyasa links postures through breath and movement. Mysore style is its traditional teaching format: you practise at your own pace while the teacher offers individual instruction within a shared room.

The sequence is learned gradually, with postures and transitions adapted to your mobility, strength and experience. Precise verbal guidance, corrections and appropriate adjustments help you understand where to work and how to progress.

The practice brings together posture, steady breathing and a focused gaze, known as Tristhana. Complete beginners and established practitioners can learn alongside one another; no knowledge of the sequence is needed to begin.

  • 01Breath establishes the rhythm of the practice.
  • 02Posture is developed with precision and patience.
  • 03A chosen gaze point helps keep attention with the practice.

Who it is for

For beginners and regular practitioners.

  • Complete beginners who want to learn from the foundation
  • Established practitioners seeking attentive, individual guidance
  • Students who want to learn the sequence at their own pace
  • People who want to develop a regular, independent practice
